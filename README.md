# 🪨📄✂️ Rock Paper Scissors: Battle Edition

## 🎮 About the Game

Rock Paper Scissors: Battle Edition is a strategy-based battle game that expands on the traditional game of Rock, Paper, Scissors by introducing upgrades, an in-game currency system, and special abilities.

Players compete in battles, earn coins through victories, upgrade their equipment, and purchase special abilities to gain an advantage over their opponents. The ultimate objective is to accumulate a specific amount of coins to win the game.

This game is a collaborative programming project developed by a team of four university students at the University of Texas at San Antonio (UTSA).

## ⚔️ Game Features

- **Battle System:** Compete using Rock, Paper, and Scissors.
- **Upgrade System:** Upgrade Rock, Paper, and Scissors to improve your battle capabilities.
- **Currency System:** Earn coins by winning battles.
- **In-Game Shop:** Spend earned coins on upgrades and special abilities.
- **Special Abilities:** Unlock additional abilities to gain strategic advantages.
- **Progression System:** Become stronger as you advance through the game.
- **Victory Condition:** Reach the required amount of coins to win.

## 🕹️ How to Play

1. Select Rock, Paper, or Scissors.
2. Compete against your opponent in battle.
3. Win battles to earn coins.
4. Visit the shop to purchase upgrades and special abilities.
5. Develop strategies to improve your chances of winning.
6. Accumulate enough coins to achieve victory.

## 🧠 Strategy

Unlike traditional Rock, Paper, Scissors, this game introduces resource management and strategic decision-making.

Players must decide whether to save their coins to reach the victory requirement or spend them on upgrades and special abilities to increase their chances of winning future battles.
